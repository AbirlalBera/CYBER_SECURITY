Step 1: Concept ko apni language me samjho

Sabse pehle ensure karo ki student definition ko meaning-wise samjhta ho.
Example:

🔹 Question: What is phishing?
🔹 Student samjhe: “Fake email ya message bhejkar user se credentials lena.”

Agar meaning clear hai, tab English me bolna easy ban jaata hai.
Concept clarity bina English me bolne ki koshish karoge to students “rote learning” karenge — aur interview me atak jaate hain.

🗣️ Step 2: 3-layer definition bolne ka habit

 ek fixed pattern me definition dena:

1. Start – Basic meaning
2. Middle – Technical explanation
3. End – Example

Example:

Phishing is a type of social engineering attack where attackers send fake emails or messages to trick users into sharing sensitive information, such as passwords or credit card numbers.
For example, an email pretending to be from a bank asking to verify your account.

Is pattern se unka answer structured aur natural lagta hai.

🔁 Step 3: “Speak & Record” practice (daily 10 mins)

Har din ek topic lo (e.g., Firewall, IDS, Encryption)

Pehle apni language me explain karo (2 min)

Fir English me bolo aur record karo (2 min)

Record suno aur check karo ki kaunse words repeat ho rahe hain ya galat highlighter hain

Agle din wahi improve karo
