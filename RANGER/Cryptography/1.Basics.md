What is the standard required for handling credit card information?

==`PCI DSS`==  :  Payment Card Industry Digital Security Standard (PCI DSS). An information security standard administered by the Payment Card Industry Security Standards Council that is for organizations that handle branded credit cards from the major card schemes.
