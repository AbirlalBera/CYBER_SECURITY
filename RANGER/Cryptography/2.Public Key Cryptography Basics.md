### **RSA :**

RSA is a method for encrypting data using two keys: one to lock (encrypt) and another to unlock (decrypt) it, relying on the difficulty of factoring large number. It is a public-key encryption algorithm that enables secure data transmission over insecure channels.

The public key is known to all correspondents and is used for encryption, while the private key is protected and used for decryption, as shown in the figure below.
